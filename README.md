# World Map of Business: corrections

Spotted something wrong on [worldmapofbusiness.com](https://worldmapofbusiness.com)? A wrong founding year, a missing CEO, a company under the wrong country? Open a correction here, ideally from the "Suggest a correction" link on the company or person page, which fills in the details for you.

**How it works**

1. You open an issue with the record, the field, what it says now, what it should say, and a source.
2. An editor reviews it and labels it `approved` or `rejected`, with a reason.
3. Approved corrections go into the next monthly data refresh, and the issue is labelled `applied` and closed.

**What makes a good correction**

- One fact per issue.
- A source we can check: an annual report, a filing, a reputable news article or Wikipedia with its own citation.
- No personal information about private individuals.

The site's facts come from Wikidata (CC0) and Wikipedia (CC BY-SA 4.0). Where a fact is wrong at the source, we also encourage fixing it there.
